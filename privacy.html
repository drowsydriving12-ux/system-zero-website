PRIVACY POLICY

This policy explains how information is handled when you use SYSTEM//ZERO and contact support.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. OVERVIEW

SYSTEM//ZERO is a gamified personal progression application for organizing quests, goals, routines, and progression. Version 1 does not create a SYSTEM//ZERO user account and does not operate a server for gameplay data.

2. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Where applicable, SYSTEM//ZERO stores your player name, XP, level, rank and progression information, attributes and stats, streak, quests, custom quest names and descriptions, categories, quest ordering, completion state, selected System, selected themes, onboarding state, local premium-entitlement cache, and application settings locally on your device using Hive and application storage.

This information is used to provide the app's features, display your progress, remember your choices, and keep your local game state. SYSTEM//ZERO does not upload this gameplay information to its own server.

Local information is generally protected by the operating system's application sandbox. SYSTEM//ZERO does not currently add a separate encryption layer to its Hive databases.

3. PROFILE IMAGE

You may choose a profile image using your device's gallery or photo picker. The selected image is processed and stored locally in the app's storage. SYSTEM//ZERO does not upload it to its own server or cloud storage. The operating system's photo picker may handle your selection under the platform provider's own controls and policies.

4. LOCAL STORAGE AND DATA LOSS

SYSTEM//ZERO does not currently provide cloud backup or cloud recovery for gameplay progress. Uninstalling the app, clearing its application data, losing or replacing the device, storage corruption, or some device failures may remove quests, XP, stats, streaks, settings, and other local progression.

Restore Purchases is separate: it may restore eligible premium System Packs from the relevant platform store, but it does not restore quests or gameplay progression.

5. PURCHASES AND RESTORATION

Solo Level Up and Knight Protocol are optional premium System Packs offered as non-consumable in-app purchases. Apple App Store / StoreKit processes purchases on iOS. Google Play Billing processes purchases on Android. SYSTEM//ZERO may store a local record that a System Pack is unlocked.

Apple or Google may process transaction information, platform-store account information, product identifiers, device and network information, and other information needed to process or restore purchases under their own policies. SYSTEM//ZERO does not receive or store your complete credit- or debit-card details.

Restore Purchases asks the relevant platform store to confirm ownership. Restoration normally requires access to the Apple App Store or Google Play account that owns the purchase and depends on the provider's records and availability. Apple purchases do not automatically transfer to Google Play, and Google Play purchases do not automatically transfer to Apple.

6. COLLECTION, ANALYTICS, AND TRACKING

SYSTEM//ZERO version 1 does not operate an advertising network, behavioral tracking system, or app analytics service. The app does not intentionally collect advertising identifiers, precise or coarse GPS location, contacts, microphone recordings, camera images, or health information.

This does not mean that no technical information ever leaves your device. Apple and Google may process IP addresses, request times, app or device identifiers, network information, and related technical data when providing their app-store and billing services. Ordinary internet and support-email services may also process technical information necessary to deliver those communications.

7. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit processes iOS purchases and purchase restoration.
  • Google Play / Google Play Billing processes Android purchases and purchase restoration.

These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. SYSTEM//ZERO does not sell personal information.

8. SUPPORT COMMUNICATIONS

If you email support, your email provider and ours process your email address, message, and attachments to deliver and answer the request. Please do not send passwords, complete payment-card details, store credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information.

9. DATA REMOVAL

SYSTEM//ZERO version 1 does not create a SYSTEM//ZERO user account, so there is no SYSTEM//ZERO account to delete and no account-deletion request is required.

You can generally remove local information by using relevant in-app reset or removal functions where available, removing selected local content, or uninstalling the application or clearing its application data, subject to your platform's behavior. These actions do not delete purchase history or other records controlled by Apple or Google. See Data & Deletion for details.

10. SECURITY

Local app storage uses protections provided by the mobile operating system, while store transactions use protections provided by Apple or Google. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

11. CHILDREN

SYSTEM//ZERO is not intended to knowingly process children's personal information in violation of applicable law. Contact support if you believe information has been handled improperly.

12. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you may have privacy rights concerning information processed when you contact support or use platform services. Apple and Google handle requests concerning information under their control. Contact us for questions about information SYSTEM//ZERO controls.

13. CHANGES

This policy may be updated as the app, platform services, or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when this happens.
