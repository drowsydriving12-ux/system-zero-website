LOST LOCAL PROGRESS

SYSTEM//ZERO stores gameplay progress locally and does not currently provide cloud backup. Progress may not be recoverable after uninstalling the app, clearing app data, device loss or replacement, storage corruption, or some device failures.

03

RESTORE PURCHASES

Use RESTORE PURCHASES in the app while using the same Apple App Store or Google Play account that owns the purchase. This can restore eligible System Packs, not quests or gameplay progress.
